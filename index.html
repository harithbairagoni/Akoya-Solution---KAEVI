Our Perspective

The answer is a better hiring operating model, not a bigger pile of unvetted CVs.

THE FOUR SYSTEMIC FRICTIONS
01

Rare skill combinations

Technical depth + industry context + business judgment. Upstream GCC mandates require practitioners who can communicate with global business stakeholders, not isolated coders.

02

Speed vs. confidence

Hiring managers need progress without compromising the bar. Rushed screening leads to high post-joining attrition; protracted processes lose top talent to competing global offers.

03

Offer-to-join uncertainty

Compensation, counteroffers, notice period vulnerability and intent matter early. Unvetted candidates routinely accept multiple offers during Indian 60–90 day notice periods.

04

Unpredictable ramp-ups

Capacity must flex as enterprise priorities, budgets, and product roadmaps change. Inflexible recruitment mechanisms cannot handle sudden, high-urgency squad spikes.

GOVERNANCE & COMPLIANCE

Enterprise Governance Protocols

1. Data Privacy & Confidentiality

KAEVI Global Workforce Partners operates in full compliance with applicable Indian information technology and personal data protection regulations, including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDPA 2023), alongside global client standards.

Candidate profiles, executive search calibration criteria, and organizational structures are treated with strict confidentiality. No candidate profile is presented without candidate permission, and no client organizational details are shared beyond screened practitioners.

2. Terms of Engagement

Client advisory, executive search mandates, contract IT staffing, and RPO engagements are governed by mutually executed Master Services Agreements (MSAs), Statements of Work (SOWs), and candidate placement schedules.

All delivery milestones, commercial fees, exclusivity parameters, and warranty replacement terms are specified formally within individual client engagement documentation.
